# Privacy Policy — Sumi for Supernote

The privacy policy for Sumi for Supernote on iPhone and iPad is published at
**<https://schaefer-dev.de/sumi/privacy/>**.

In short: the developer collects no data. The app has no accounts, servers, analytics or tracking.
Dropbox access is read-only and goes directly between your device and Dropbox. Handwriting
recognition runs on the device.

The macOS apps and Quick Look extension work entirely offline.

Questions: [sumi@schaefer-dev.de](mailto:sumi@schaefer-dev.de)
